        <h3 class="project-title">Brightsky</h3>
        <p class="project-text">
        Brightsky is a large research initiative focused on improving the innovation capacity and competitiveness of the Dutch aviation sector. Within Brightsky, the DMC group is active in the Maintenance, Repair & Overhaul (MRO) work package. In this work package, a DMC PhD candidate developed a system for monitoring the cold spray process based on the emitted sound, and in addition supported other parters in the development of a qualified cold spray repair. Within Brightsky, the DMC group works together with the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences, EPCOR, KLM Engineering & Maintenance, and the NLR.

        <h3 class="project-title"> COCOLIH2T (Clean Hydrogen Joint Undertaking) </h3>
        <p class="project-text">
        The COCOLIH2T project is working to develop a conformal composite liquid hydrogen tank suitable for aviation. Within COCOLIH2T, DMC group members worked on the preliminary safety analysis for the tank, as well as predicting it’s durability and fatigue behaviour, particularly at cryogenic conditions. Within COCOLIH2T, the DMC group collaborates with ATR, Collins Aerospace, Novotech, NLR and Unified International.

        <h3 class="project-title"> CSAR (NWO - OTP) </h3>
        <p class="project-text">
        In the CSAR project, DMC researchers are working on understanding the fatigue and impact resistance of cold spray repairs on aluminium alloys. CSAR is supported by a User Committee consisting of ADSE, KLM Engineering & Maintenance,  SAM XL, and Titomic.

        <h3 class="project-title"> D-STANDART (Horizon Europe) </h3>
        <p class="project-text">
        The D-STANDART project focused on improving our ability to characterize and predict the fatigue behaviour of composite laminates. Within D-STANDART, DMC group members worked on investigating the effect of fibre orientation and of temperature and humidity on delamination growth. Within D-STANDART, we collaborated with Hexagon, iCOMAT, L-UP, the National Composites Centre, NLR, Suzlon, the University of Bristol, and the University of Twente.
        </p>

        <h3 class="project-title"> Luchtvaart in Transitie (National Growth Fund) </h3>
        <p class="project-text">
        Luchtvaart in Transitie (LiT) is a large Dutch programme enabling the transition to sustainable aviation. DMC researchers are active within a number of LiT projects, working on understanding the effect of manufacturing defects and variability in composite materials, and investigating how to more efficiently characterise the fatigue performance of aerospace materials. Within LiT, the DMC group is working with GKN Aerospace.
