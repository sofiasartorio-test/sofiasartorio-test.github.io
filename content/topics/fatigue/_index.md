        <h2> Fracture and Fatigue of Composite Structures </h2>

        <p> Composites offer a lot of design freedom by allowing designers to precisely tailor the make-up of the material through varying the lay-up. However, this design freedom introduces a lot of complexity to the material’s damage behaviour. At present the solution is to treat each lay-up as its own material, necessitating very expensive and extensive test efforts to certify composite structures. 

        Within the DMC group we seek to better understand how damage behaviour is affected by changes in the lay-up, as well as by other features such as manufacturing defects. Our goal is to minimize the amount of testing required to predict the performance of composite structures, by instead relying on models grounded in physical understanding of the material behaviour, rather than empirical calibration. 
        </p>
