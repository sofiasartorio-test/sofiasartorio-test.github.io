        <h2> Evaluation, Qualification, and Certification of Cold Spray Repairs </h2>

        <p> Cold spray is a material deposition technology that is very promising for repair of aircraft due to its ability of adding material on an existing substrate with minimal heat input. This makes it uniquely suitable for repair of non-weldable alloys, such as the aluminium alloys commonly used in aerospace. In addition, the flexibility and potential portability of commercial cold spray systems also opens the door to developing ‘on wing’  repairs, that can replace more time consuming alternative methods.

        However, in order to use cold spray repairs in a safety critical environment such as aerospace, we need to be able to guarantee the performance and structural integrity of the repair, including its long term durability. This is complicated by the large range of process parameters than can influence the deposit quality, and by the fact that each repair is unique.
        </p>

        <div class="text-azzurro">
        <p>
        Within the DMC group we are therefore developing methodologies that combine experimental data and modelling to predict the performance of a cold spray repair before it is applied, and to better understand what process parameters to monitor to ensure the quality of the repair.
        </p>
        </div>
